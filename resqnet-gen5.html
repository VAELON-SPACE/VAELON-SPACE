Introducing the next generation of ResQNet

Gen 5

More intelligence. More control. A safer tomorrow.

ResQNet Gen 5 is the next major version of the routing and decision engine, targeting a January 2027 launch. It carries the same risk-weighted routing and max-flow allocation core forward, then adds AI/ML-driven decisioning, a much larger node capacity ceiling, and a smoother, faster real-time experience end to end. Pricing and full spec sheet will be announced closer to launch.

What's New

What Gen 5 adds

Gen 5 builds on the same risk-weighted routing and max-flow allocation core that runs in Gen 4 today, then extends it in a few specific directions.

Intelligence

AI/ML-integrated decisioning

Machine-learning models sit alongside the existing routing and allocation core to strengthen how decisions are scored and explained.

Scale

Higher node capacity

The graph core is being rebuilt to hold and route across a substantially larger network — more nodes, more concurrent incidents.

Performance

A smoother real-time experience

Faster response through the interface and the decision loop itself, so operators aren't waiting on the system under pressure.

Coordination

One shared operational view

Field teams, operations and decision-makers work from the same live picture instead of reconciling separate reports.

Continuity

Same core, carried forward

The risk-weighted shortest-path and max-flow allocation engine validated in Gen 4 remains the foundation — Gen 5 extends it, it doesn't replace it.

Roadmap

And more, ahead of launch

Additional functionality is still being finalized. The full spec sheet and pricing will be announced closer to the January 2027 launch.

Availability

Who Gen 5 is for

Access for Gen 5 works differently from Gen 4, by design. Pricing is not final and will be announced closer to launch.

Non-Licensed / Licensed Developer Tiers
Not offered for Gen 5

The individual-developer source tiers ($200 one-time / $500 per year) that exist for Gen 4 will not carry over to Gen 5.

  • No individual-developer source access
  • No code-reviewer tier
  • Discontinued following a prior source-leak and misuse incident on the individual-developer tiers
Organization / Government
Pricing TBA

Gen 5 — and future updated models beyond it — will be distributed only through B2B and organization/government contracts.

  • Full spec sheet and pricing announced closer to January 2027
  • Contract-based deployment, no public source distribution
  • Early enterprise interest can be registered now
Register enterprise interest

Why this changed. Gen 4's Non-Licensed and Licensed individual-developer source tiers previously saw a leak and misuse incident on the developer side. To close that exposure going forward, Gen 5 and later updated models will not be sold as individual-developer source access — they will be available exclusively to organizations and companies under contract. Gen 4's existing developer tiers are unaffected and continue to operate as described on the Gen 4 licensing page.
