SEC 06 / Deep Space

Designed for the deep space problem

Long-duration missions expose every weakness in propulsion, thermal protection, power, navigation and system reliability. Mars is the benchmark Vaelon Space designs against.

Long-duration propulsion

Electric and staged-combustion systems engineered for efficiency over years, not minutes, of continuous burn.

Thermal protection

Ablative and multi-layer insulation architectures for high-energy atmospheric entry.

Autonomous navigation

Star-tracker guidance and rendezvous logic for missions beyond real-time ground contact.

Mission reliability

Design margins and redundancy planning for a mission with zero room for failure.
