SEC 00 / Deep Space

Making Vaelon multiplanetary

Mars is the clearest proof point for what long-duration propulsion has to survive — years of transit, thin atmosphere entry, and zero margin for failure. It's the benchmark Vaelon Space designs against.

Why Mars?

A 480-million-kilometer round trip is the clearest proof point for high-ISP, long-duration propulsion architecture.

Advanced propulsion

Electric and staged-combustion systems engineered for efficiency over years, not minutes, of continuous burn.

Future exploration

Research tracks in orbital architecture and autonomous navigation aimed at interplanetary-class missions.
